LiDAR-Camera Calibration — Session Report
Date: 2026-04-06 | Sensors: Ouster LiDAR + Insta360 omnidirectional camera

Background
Baseline calibration using the koide3 direct_visual_lidar_calibration toolbox showed visible misalignment when coloring the LiDAR point cloud with the camera image — a ring-shaped "ghosting" artifact surrounding foreground objects.

Problem Investigation
Ghosting Artifact
The colored point cloud shows a halo of incorrectly colored points around objects. Initial suspicion was FOV mismatch: the LiDAR has 360° × 45° FOV, the Insta360 has ~210°. SuperPoint runs on the full camera image, producing keypoints in regions the LiDAR never sees. These feed geometrically inconsistent matches into the RANSAC initial guess.

However, investigation revealed the primary cause is physical parallax from the ~10 cm vertical offset between sensors:

Range	Parallax angle	Pixel displacement
1 m	5.7°	~70 px
5 m	1.1°	~14 px
10 m	0.57°	~7 px
LiDAR wall points behind an object are correctly placed in 3D by the calibration, but the camera's higher viewpoint places the object's edge pixels at that location in the image. This is physics, not calibration error. The toolbox's example results look better because they use outdoor scenes at 10+ m range and pinhole cameras with narrow FOV — both conditions minimize parallax impact. Our indoor lab scene at 1–3 m makes parallax the dominant effect.

Attempted Fix: FOV Intersection Masking
Approach: Restrict SuperGlue feature matching to only the region where both sensor FOVs genuinely overlap, filtering out camera keypoints the LiDAR cannot see.

Two tools were built:

fov_visualizer.py — Interactive side-by-side viewer: camera image with adjustable circular arc boundaries (upper/lower LiDAR elevation limits projected into the fisheye), and the pre-rendered LiDAR equirectangular panorama with elevation/azimuth cut sliders. Exports parameters to fov_params.json.

find_matches_superglue_masked.py — Modified SuperGlue script that reads fov_params.json and zeros out both images outside the overlap region before SuperPoint runs.

Outcome: Marginal improvement. The masking only affects the SuperGlue initial guess step. The dominant calibrate step (NID photometric optimizer) is naturally LiDAR-driven — it only touches pixels where LiDAR points project — so camera-only-visible regions don't corrupt it regardless of masking. RANSAC in initial_guess_auto was already filtering most bad matches.

Side effect discovered: The RGB image support added earlier broke the NID optimizer, which assumed single-channel input. image.at<uint8_t>() on a BGR image reads only the blue channel, corrupting the joint histogram. Fixed by adding a grayscale conversion step before NID computation.

Alternative Methods Investigated
CoFiI2P (Coarse-to-Fine Image-to-Point Cloud, RA-L 2024) was evaluated as a replacement for the SuperGlue step. It directly matches raw image features to 3D point cloud features without the intermediate LiDAR→2D reprojection, which would eliminate the equirectangular panorama step entirely. The feature matching stages are camera-model agnostic; only the final EPnP solver assumes pinhole, which could be replaced with the existing omnidir solver in the toolbox. The practical limitation is domain gap — the pretrained models were trained on KITTI/NuScenes pinhole images and may generalize poorly to fisheye images. Not pursued further for now.

LightGlue was identified as the most practical near-term improvement — a drop-in replacement for SuperGlue with 2–4× faster inference and better match recall. Not yet implemented.

Open Issues
Issue	Notes
Ghosting at close range	Fundamental parallax. Could be reduced by depth-buffer occlusion culling in the viewer (don't color a LiDAR point if a closer point projects to the same pixel). Logic already exists in ViewCulling for the calibrate step but not applied in the viewer.
Indoor calibration accuracy	Targetless NID calibration was designed for outdoor, long-range scenes with rich texture. A physical calibration target visible to both sensors may be more reliable for close-range indoor use.
